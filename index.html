How to read this table

  • Benchmark hours = fixed hours + hours per unit × units. Units come from your Inputs (e.g. 10 locations × 1 round = 10 units). Open “Benchmark” under an activity to see how its hours are built.
  • If you know your actual effort, enter it in “Your estimate” – it replaces the benchmark. Activities outside the selected modules count as 0.
  • Expected reductions reflect what remains with FI: preparation, review, exception handling and administration. Human review and approval remain part of every workflow.
  • *Applied reduction = reduction of the selected scenario × (1 − share already covered by your current tool), so existing capabilities are not credited twice.
  • Saved hours are released capacity. They only reduce cash costs if they replace staffing or external spend.
  • Only the “Expected” reduction is entered per activity. Conservative and Optimistic apply the scenario factors (capped at 95%).

Additional value – not included in the figures above

  • Reuse across frameworks: the same data serves CSRD/ESRS, VSME, CDP, EcoVadis and customer requests without re-collecting it.
  • Scalability: more entities, locations and reporting requests without a proportional increase in workload.
  • Tool consolidation: further software, databases or services may be replaced – only include them above where cancellation is realistic.
  • Decarbonization: identification and prioritization of reduction measures, including their financial implications.
  • Management decisions: faster access to consistent, traceable sustainability figures.
  • Risk and assurance: audit trail and central evidence reduce the risk of findings and rework.

These effects are real but customer-specific. Add them to the business case only where you can substantiate them.

Key modelling principles

  • Equivalent scope: today's effort and the FI scenario cover the same modules, entities and frameworks.
  • No double counting: capabilities already provided by your current tool are credited and each activity is counted once.
  • Capacity vs. cash: saved time is shown as released capacity; only reduced external spend is a cash saving.
  • Human review: AI-assisted steps keep review and approval with your team – the remaining hours with FI include this effort.
  • Factor updates preserve reporting-year consistency; regulatory support supplements but does not replace your own assessment.
  • All figures are estimates based on your inputs and typical benchmarks. They are not a guarantee of savings.
